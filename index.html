What we build

Three systems that replace the next hire on your roadmap.

01

Automated branded reports.

Client-facing performance reports that build, brand, and ship themselves. White-labeled to your agency, regenerated on a schedule, delivered straight into your client's inbox. The version of "reporting" that doesn't require a Monday-morning analyst.

Pulls from Triple Whale, Hyros, Meta, Google, GA4, server-side conversions (whatever data your clients care about), and writes the narrative around it.

Replaces Reporting analyst Looker Studio babysitting "Send me the deck"
02

AI anomaly monitoring.

An AI that watches every paid account, every funnel, every attribution model, and tells you when something quietly broke. Spend doubled three days ago? Conversion rate drifting on the new audience? You hear about it before the client does.

Replaces the daily-dashboard-scroll ritual. Replaces "I should have caught that on Tuesday." Replaces the senior account manager who used to notice these things.

Replaces Daily dashboard scroll Senior account manager pattern-matching Monday-morning surprises
03

Custom AI chat layer.

Drop a chat interface into your client portal, or into Slack, that actually knows their data. Your clients ask, "Why did CAC spike last week?" and get a real answer drawn from their pixel, their CRM, their ad accounts. Not a generic LLM. Their numbers.

Built on the same architecture we use for industrial operators querying live sensor telemetry, then re-engineered for marketing performance.
