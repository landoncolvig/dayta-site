## What the evidence does not establish

North has 67 average FTE versus 70 planned, and 18 late projects out of 120 completed versus 8 out of 130 planned (staffing.json, S-N). That is a 15.0% actual late-project rate versus 6.2% budget. These observations do not prove staffing caused lower revenue. Billing timing, engagement mix, project-level hours, and revenue-recognition detail were not supplied. Contractor spending is higher, but the record does not establish whether it was unnecessary.
